Helper robot

You are not building a real robot with motors. You are making a small cardboard helper that can pinch, push, or scoop a plastic bottle lid.

What it looks like when you’re done Two cardboard “fingers,” pipe cleaners for the bendy joints, a name on it. You squeeze or push — the fingers grab the lid (the little plastic cap), not a full water bottle.

On your table

Cardboard, tape, scissors, pipe cleaners, a plastic bottle lid (the small cap). A cup if you want. Teams of 3. No rubber bands. No full water bottle.
