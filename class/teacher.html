Engineering supplies

What they build: cardboard tongs / helper. Pipe cleaners are the joints. It pinches or pushes a plastic bottle lid (the little cap). Not a motor robot. Not a full water bottle.

Per team of 3: cardboard, tape, scissors, pipe cleaners, one plastic lid. A cup is optional.

Demo: two cardboard strips, pipe cleaner twisted at one end so they open and close. Grab a lid. That’s a pass.

Turn in: they click Submit my work and paste into a Classroom Question. You see it in that class. You look at the robot yourself.

Day-by-day

Engineering Day 1: Who is it for, one job, score 3 ideas, how it works, start building the joint.
Day 2: 17-min build, 5 tries (need 3 yeses), show the class.

Careers Day 1: 12 yes/no questions, pick a job family, 3 jobs.
Day 2: strongest job in Texas, one goal they can actually do this year, 60-second talk.

Work skills Day 1: hello, ready-or-not, put 6 jobs in order.
Day 2: you still need 20 index cards for a dropped order (pull one). Site holds the problem, email, and talk.
